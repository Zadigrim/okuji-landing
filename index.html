Okuji

A curiosity platform for people who pay attention.

Okuji helps people collect verified stamps at meaningful places, keep journals of what they noticed, and produce permanent records of their experiences.

The name comes from Bashō's Narrow Road to Oku, the founding text of careful travel writing. We're building the modern infrastructure for that kind of attention.

The platform is in development. If you're a teacher, librarian, ranger, museum educator, or institutional partner who wants to know more, reach out.
