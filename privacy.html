okuji — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 11, 2026
Provider: okuji (“we,” “us”)
Contact: nathan@okuji.app

1. Who we are and what this covers

okuji is a digital passport platform. People (“collectors”) acquire themed passports, travel to real-world locations, earn verified stamps, and keep a private journal of what they experienced. This policy explains what data the okuji mobile app collects, why, how it is stored, and the choices you have.

This policy covers the okuji mobile application. Our companion web platform and creator tools are governed by their own terms.

2. Our privacy commitments, in plain language

okuji is built so that privacy is enforced by how the system is engineered, not only by promises. The core commitments:

  • We do not track your location in the background. The app accesses your location only at the moment you place a stamp — never before, never after, never while the app is closed.
  • We do not store your precise location. Your precise coordinates are used once, momentarily, to confirm you are near a stamp location, and are then discarded. Only a coarse, area-level approximation is kept.
  • We do not sell your data. Ever. To anyone.
  • We do not show you ads and we do not use advertising or tracking software development kits (SDKs).
  • We do not read your journal for analytics. We never analyze the content you write or the entries you read.
  • We never use data generated by children for advertising, and never license or sell it.

The sections below describe exactly how each of these works.

3. What we collect and why

3.1 Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and authentication credentials, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase). This is used to sign you in, sync your passports across your devices, and contact you about your account. We use email/password and optional Google sign-in.

3.2 Precise location — used momentarily, not stored

When you place a stamp at a location-based stop, the app requests your device’s precise location at that moment only.

Your precise coordinates are sent to our verification function, used to confirm you are within range of the stamp location, and then discarded.

What we retain with your stamp is only a geohash — a coarse, area-level approximation (in our current implementation, roughly a 1.2 km × 0.6 km cell). This is enough to know the general area, and not enough to reconstruct your exact position.

The app requests foreground (in-use) location only. It never requests background location access and cannot access your location while it is closed or not in active use.

A separate, optional “near me” discovery feature may use your device’s momentary location to show nearby passports. When used, that location is used only to run the query and is not stored or logged.

3.3 Photos and audio — your journal

okuji lets you attach photos and record voice journal entries to your stamps. To do this the app requests access to your camera and microphone when you choose to use those features. This media is content you create, stored as part of your private journal (see Section 4). We do not analyze it.

3.4 User content — stamps and journals

Your stamps, journal text, mood ratings, and the passports you acquire are stored so your collection persists and syncs across your devices.

3.5 What we do NOT collect

We do not collect: your contacts, your background location, your browsing or app activity outside okuji, advertising identifiers, or any data through third-party advertising or analytics SDKs (we do not use them).

4. How your data is protected

  • Row-level security. Our database enforces, at the database level, that you can read only your own stamps, journals, and account data. This is not an application-layer promise; it is enforced in the data store itself.
  • Encryption in transit. All communication between the app and our servers uses standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Encryption at rest. Stored data is encrypted at rest by our infrastructure provider.
  • Journal content. We do not read or analyze your journal content for any purpose.
  • Operational analytics are minimal and content-free. We record only operational events necessary to run the product (for example, that a stamp was earned or a passport completed). We never instrument or analyze what you write or read.

5. Children’s privacy

Some okuji collectors are minors who participate through schools, libraries, museums, or other institutions, or through family use.

  • No advertising, ever, on anyone’s data — and absolutely not on children’s. Data generated by users under 18 is never used for advertising, never licensed, and never sold.
  • We apply heightened protection to data generated by minors and limit its use to operating the product for the person who generated it.
  • For use through an educational or institutional context, the institution is the party that introduces minors to the platform, and additional protections and agreements apply in that setting.

6. How you can access and delete your data

Account deletion. You can delete your account and all associated data from within the okuji app at any time (Profile → Danger Zone → Delete account), or request deletion at okuji.app/delete-account.

What deletion does. When you delete your account, your drafts and unshared content are deleted. Passports you have acquired that are preserved for other holders may be retained in a delisted, custodial form to honor those holders’ access; this limited retention is the only exception and exists solely to preserve other people’s collections, never for our benefit.

Access. You can contact us at nathan@okuji.app to ask what data we hold about you.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or license your personal data. We share data only with the infrastructure providers necessary to run the service (for example, our hosting, authentication, and storage provider), who process it on our behalf under their own security obligations, and only as needed to operate okuji. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers, because we work with none.

8. Data retention

  • Account and collection data is retained while your account is active.
  • Precise location is never retained (Section 3.2).
  • On account deletion, data is removed as described in Section 6, with the single custodial exception noted there.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date and, for material changes, notify you within the app or by email.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: nathan@okuji.app
