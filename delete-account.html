Delete your okuji account

For the okuji mobile app by okuji (com.okuji.app).

You can delete your okuji account and its associated data at any time, by either method below. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

Open okuji → Profile tab → Danger Zone → Delete account → confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — By request

Email privacy@okuji.app from the email address on your okuji account with the subject “Account deletion request.” We will verify the request and delete the account, confirming by reply when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your journal entries, photos, and voice recordings.
  • Your stamps, collection, and passport acquisitions.
  • Your profile information.

What may be retained

If you created passports that other collectors have acquired, those passports may be retained in a delisted, custodial form solely to preserve those collectors’ access to their own collections. This is the only exception, as described in our privacy policy. Precise location is never stored at all (it is used momentarily during stamping and discarded), so there is none to delete.

Questions

Anything unclear, or want to know what data we hold before deciding? Email privacy@okuji.app.
